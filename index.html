The Process

Simple as That.

01
You Call
Tell us what you have — how many wrenches, what range, and where you're located. We'll give you a price and schedule a pickup time.
02
We Pick Up
We come to your shop and collect your tools. No shipping, no boxes, no driving across town. Free within 5 miles of Kuna. Just $1/mile beyond that.
03
We Calibrate
Every wrench tested against NIST-traceable reference standards per ASME B107.300. Adjusted where needed. Documented on a certificate.
04
We Drop Off
Within 24 hours — usually less. We return your tools with a calibration certificate for each one. Stickered, certified, ready to work.

Can't do local? Shipping to us is also available — send your tools in, we calibrate and ship them back with certificates. Contact us to arrange.

Common Questions

FAQ

Do I need to bring my tools anywhere? +
No. We come to your shop, pick up your wrenches, calibrate them, and bring them back. You don't leave, you don't ship anything, and you don't stop working.
How long does it take? +
24 hours or less from pickup to drop-off. Most jobs come back same day. Rush turnaround available for time-sensitive situations (additional charge applies).
What do I get with each calibration? +
Each tool comes back with a calibration certificate showing the test results, reference standard used, next due date, and technician signature. A calibration sticker goes on the tool. Certificates are emailed and available in your Google Drive folder.
Are your calibrations traceable? +
Yes. All calibrations are performed using reference standards traceable to NIST. Methodology follows ASME B107.300.
What torque range do you cover? +
We calibrate all torque tools up to 600 ft-lb at standard rates. Tools over 600 ft-lb require a custom quote — call us before booking so we can confirm scope and pricing.
What areas do you serve? +
We serve the full Treasure Valley — Kuna, Nampa, Caldwell, Meridian, Boise, Eagle, Star, and surrounding areas. Pickup and drop-off is free within 5 miles of Kuna. Beyond 5 miles we charge $1 per mile.
Do you offer fleet contracts or annual programs? +
Yes. Annual service agreements are available for fleets with recurring calibration needs. 25+ tools qualifies for 20% volume pricing. Contact us to discuss a contract that fits your schedule and budget.
